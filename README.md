The TARUMT Asset Management System is a web-based, zero-installation platform designed to modernize physical asset tracking, maintenance scheduling, and fiscal reporting across campus facilities.

By combining QR-code identification, a dynamic Priority Matrix Algorithm, and financial analytics, the platform addresses operational delays caused by manual reporting and eliminates "ghost assets".

1. 🏷️ QR-Identity & Inventory Control
- Unique QR Code Generation: Automatically generates QR IDs linked to physical equipment, locations, purchase dates, and warranties.
- Instant Browser Lookup: Allows students and staff to scan assets via standard mobile camera apps without requiring native app installation.

2. ⚡ Priority-Driven Maintenance Engine
- Dynamic Priority Scoring: Automatically ranks repair tickets upon submission using a weighted algorithm
- No-Login Guest Reporting: Enables friction-free fault submission for students to increase campus incident reporting rates.
- Visual Proof Verification: Enforces photo evidence attachments during reporting and repair completion.

3. 📊 AI-Enabled Financial Analytics
- Cost Aggregation: Automatically tracks repair expenses per asset, room, block, and faculty.
- Budget Projections: Leverages predictive analysis to forecast upcoming semester maintenance expenditure.
